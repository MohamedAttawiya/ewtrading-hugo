# Alkyd Resin Export RFQ

Use this page when you are ready to request commercial terms for alkyd resin supply.

## What to include

- Grade or family (long, medium, short, urethane-modified)
- Application and target properties
- Quantity in MT and delivery cadence
- Destination city/country and preferred Incoterm
- Required documentation (TDS, COA, SDS)
